Good to know

Questions Families Often Ask

What type of newborn care does Beverly offer?

Private in-home newborn care with hands-on parent guidance, overnight newborn care, and a virtual newborn guidance program called The First Two Weeks. Every engagement is built around your baby's individual cues rather than a fixed schedule.

Is overnight care available?

Yes. Overnight care is included in the 24/7 in-home program, and it is also offered on its own as a 12-hour overnight shift.

Does Beverly travel?

Yes. The service area is worldwide, with travel available. Availability is confirmed on inquiry.

How long can in-home care last?

Families begin with a few days, one week, several weeks, or three months. Three months is often the ideal window, as it allows Beverly to guide families through the most important early developmental phases and establish sleep foundations naturally over time.

What is included in private in-home care?

An intensive schedule of 20 hours on with a four-hour daytime break: newborn care day and night, hands-on training for both parents, and the daily baby essentials — cleaning bottles, sanitizing pump parts, baby laundry, organizing feeding supplies, preparing baby essentials, and maintaining routines.

What is virtual newborn guidance?

It is Beverly's expertise delivered remotely, for families who want expert support without full-time care in the home — scheduled FaceTime consultations plus direct access by text and phone.

What is The First Two Weeks?

A two-week virtual program: a one-hour pre-birth preparation consultation, a one-hour newborn arrival consultation, and 14 days of direct access to Beverly by text, phone, and FaceTime. The Premium package adds the full video tutorial library for the first two weeks of life.

How are virtual calls scheduled?

Because Beverly is often working with an in-home family around the clock, calls and FaceTimes are scheduled by text first so she can coordinate a time and give each family undivided attention.

Can virtual families later transition to in-home support?

Yes. The virtual program is designed as both immediate support and a pathway to in-person care. Families who book in-person care after completing the virtual program receive 5% off their in-person booking.

What does in-home care cost?

24/7 services are $23 per hour for 20 hours. Night services are $35 per hour for a 12-hour overnight shift. The virtual program is $750 for the Essential package and $1,000 for the Premium package. Customized scheduling and availability are confirmed upon inquiry.

Does Beverly work with newborns who need extra attentiveness?

Her experience extends to newborns requiring a higher level of attentiveness, including liver-related concerns, heart conditions, pyloric stenosis, feeding challenges, reflux, weight-gain concerns, and post-hospital recovery. This is non-medical newborn care and parent support — it does not replace your pediatrician, physician, nurse, or hospital, and it is not medical advice.

How do I begin, and how does a consultation work?

Send a consultation request with your due date or your baby's date of birth, your location, and the support you're considering. Beverly will learn about your family and baby's timeline, then recommend the service and level of support that fits — before anything is booked.
