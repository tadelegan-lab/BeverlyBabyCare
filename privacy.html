Privacy Policy

Beverly Baby Care collects only the information families choose to share when requesting a consultation — such as name, email address, phone number, location, expected due date or baby's date of birth, and a description of the support being sought.

That information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, plan appropriate care, and communicate about your engagement. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing.

Details shared about your family, your household, and your baby are treated as private and confidential.

To ask what information is held about you, or to request its deletion, email beverlybabycare@gmail.com.

This page is a plain-language summary. Beverly Baby Care should have it reviewed against the privacy laws that apply where it operates before launch.
