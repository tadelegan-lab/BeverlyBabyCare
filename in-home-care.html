Why families choose us

Why Families Choose Private In-Home Care

There is no substitute for experienced hands inside the home during the newborn stage. Virtual guidance can offer reassurance, but in-person care allows Beverly to fully understand your baby in real time — not only what parents describe, but what the baby is quietly communicating moment by moment.

She studies the subtle details most people miss:

  • Feeding rhythm
  • Sleep transitions
  • Breathing patterns
  • Grunts & cries
  • Body tension
  • Digestion
  • Soothing responses
  • Overstimulation signs
  • Natural wake windows

This is where experience becomes invaluable — because often the smallest sounds and shifts reveal exactly what a baby needs before patterns become difficult habits.

Her ability to interpret those early cues allows families to avoid unnecessary stress while building healthy rhythms that feel natural rather than forced.

Around the clock

A True 24/7 Presence in the Home

Beverly works on an intensive newborn schedule of 20 hours on, with a four-hour daytime break, offering families an exceptional level of continuity and support.

This includes overnight care — often the most transformative part of the experience. Throughout the night, Beverly remains deeply attentive, listening closely to every sound, movement, grunt, and cry, learning your baby's language while parents are finally able to rest.

For many families, this rest becomes one of the greatest gifts of all.

Recovery matters. Sleep matters. Mental clarity matters — especially in the earliest postpartum weeks, when even one uninterrupted stretch of sleep can change how parents feel physically and emotionally.

For the whole family

Support for Mother, Father, and Baby

Beverly actively trains both parents, helping mother and father develop confidence together so both feel equally capable and connected.

Hands-on guidance includes

  • Breastfeeding support, including foods to avoid
  • Pumping support
  • Bellybutton care
  • Circumcision care
  • Burping techniques
  • Soothing methods
  • Reading hunger cues
  • Reading sleep cues
  • Diapering & bathing
  • Gentle tummy time

Daily essentials

Everything for Baby Is Handled

Beverly manages the daily newborn essentials that quietly accumulate and quickly overwhelm new parents.

  • Cleaning bottles
  • Sanitizing pump parts
  • Baby laundry
  • Organizing feeding supplies
  • Preparing baby essentials
  • Maintaining routines

Her presence brings calm, order, and consistency into a home during a season that often feels unpredictable.

Advanced expertise

Specialized Experience with High-Risk Newborns

Beverly's experience extends to caring for newborns who require a higher level of attentiveness and specialized support, including:

  • Liver-related concerns
  • Heart conditions
  • Pyloric stenosis
  • Feeding challenges
  • Reflux
  • Weight-gain concerns
  • Post-hospital recovery

Her calm presence, close observation, and ability to recognize subtle changes provide families with reassurance during especially delicate periods.

Beverly Baby Care provides non-medical newborn care and parent guidance. It is not a substitute for care from your pediatrician, physician, nurse, or hospital, and nothing here is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Families with medical concerns should always contact their healthcare provider.

Good to know

In-Home Care Questions

What does a typical day of in-home care look like?

Beverly works an intensive newborn schedule of 20 hours on with a four-hour daytime break. That covers newborn care, overnight support, parent training, and the daily baby essentials — bottles, pump parts, baby laundry, feeding supplies, and routines.

Is overnight care included?

Yes. Overnight care is part of the 24/7 program, and it is also available on its own as a 12-hour overnight shift.

How long do families usually book?

Families begin with a few days, one week, several weeks, or three months. Three months is often the ideal window, as it allows Beverly to guide families through the most important early developmental phases.

Does Beverly travel?

Yes — service area is worldwide, with travel available. Scheduling and availability are confirmed on inquiry.
