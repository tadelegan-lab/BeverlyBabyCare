The First Two Weeks

Personalized newborn guidance with Beverly, for families who want expert support without full-time care in the home.

Book a ConsultationSee Packages

“The first two weeks of a baby's life are some of the most important, emotional, and overwhelming days for new parents. Every baby arrives with their own rhythm, signals, personality, and needs.”

Program philosophy

It Begins With One Essential Truth

Your baby is an individual. Not your friend's baby, not your sister's baby, not what an app predicts. Your baby has their own DNA, their own developmental pace, and their own way of communicating.

Beverly's approach is centered around helping parents trust their baby's signals, understand their cues, and build confidence early — while also creating healthy structure that supports the wellbeing of the entire family.

A well-supported parent is a better parent: more sleep, more clarity, more patience, more engagement, and a healthier mental state during one of life's biggest transitions.

Beverly's belief

Program overview

What the Program Includes

Step 01

Pre-Birth Preparation Consultation

1 hour · FaceTime call

Before baby arrives, Beverly meets virtually with parents to prepare them properly for those first days — ensuring you feel organized, informed, and less overwhelmed before birth.

  • Newborn essentials you actually need
  • Setting feeding goals (breast, pump, bottle)
  • Preparing for postpartum realities
  • Physical & emotional expectations after delivery
  • Realistic expectations for first days home
  • Priorities based on your family's lifestyle

Step 02

Newborn Arrival Consultation

1 hour · FaceTime call

Once baby arrives, Beverly provides immediate guidance tailored to your specific newborn — helping parents learn how to read their baby, not just follow generalized advice.

  • What to expect in the first 14 days
  • Feeding frequency & adequacy signs
  • Breastfeeding support & flange sizing
  • Bottle introduction strategy
  • Monitoring diaper counts
  • Signs of dehydration
  • Understanding weight fluctuations
  • Sleep expectations
  • Gas, digestion & comfort
  • Schedule: naps, bedtime, tummy time

Step 03

Direct Access & Support for 2 Weeks

14 days · text, phone & FaceTime

For the full first two weeks, Beverly remains available for direct support. Instead of spiraling online, parents have direct access to experienced guidance for the moments that matter most.

  • “Is this normal?”
  • “Why is baby doing this?”
  • “Should I be worried?”
  • “Are we feeding enough?”

Because Beverly is often working with an in-home family 24/7, all calls and FaceTimes must first be scheduled by text so she can coordinate an appropriate time to connect — ensuring every family receives thoughtful, undivided attention.

Step 04

Video Tutorial Library

Step-by-step practical guidancePremium add-on

Parents receive access to Beverly's practical demonstrations, designed to reduce anxiety and build confidence — real-life skills you can return to anytime.

  • Holding your baby properly
  • Understanding hunger cues
  • Recognizing overtiredness
  • Gas & overstimulation cues
  • How to burp effectively
  • Tummy time & play time
  • Diaper changing techniques
  • Proper wipe-down techniques
  • Establishing healthy daily rhythm
  • Independent awake time

For families seeking more

Why In-Person Support Is the Gold Standard

While virtual guidance provides strong support, Beverly's work is most impactful in person. Her true skill comes from directly observing and understanding each baby firsthand — the nuances that only proximity reveals:

  • The sounds they make
  • Their breathing patterns
  • Their grunts
  • Their cries
  • Transitions between sleep and feeding

When working in person, Beverly typically works 20-hour days, taking only a four-hour daytime break, and remains with the baby overnight to deeply understand their rhythm and needs. This allows her to:

  • Help shape healthy patterns early
  • Support feeding directly
  • Guide parents in real time
  • Provide immediate reassurance
  • Give parents meaningful rest during recovery
  • Identify cues most parents miss early on

The virtual program is designed as both immediate support and a pathway for families who may wish to bring Beverly in person — for a few days, one week, two weeks, or longer.
